# Credit Balance

The workspace’s remaining and used AI credits, purchase allocation and allowance period.

## Information kept

- **Remaining** — The retained remaining.
- **Used** — The retained used.
- **Purchased** — The retained purchased.
- **Period Start** — The retained period start.
- **Period End** — The retained period end.
- **Workspace** — The workspace to which this instance belongs.

## States

### Available

The credit balance is available.
